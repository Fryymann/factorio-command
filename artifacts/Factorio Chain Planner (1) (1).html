Mark an item Import to bring it in on the bus instead of building it here. It then enters the layout as a raw input column.

How the layout works: raw inputs and every intermediate get their own column on a main bus on the left, flowing down. Each recipe gets a row of machines: one input belt carries two ingredients on separate lanes, a second belt with long-handed inserters carries a third and fourth, and the output belt returns to the bus and starts that item's column. Feeders split off the bus and cross it with underground belts. Rows split automatically when a lane or the output belt would be over capacity, or past the max machines per row.

Before you build it: fluids are drawn as pipe guides along each row and are left out of the blueprint, since chemical plants and fluid recipes need their pipe connections placed by hand. Connect the rows to your power network; each row has its own small poles. Inserter speeds are approximate chest-to-chest figures without stack bonus research.

Not modelled yet: modules, beacons, productivity bonuses, oil refining, foundry, electromagnetic plant, and recipes from other planets. Items tagged check are recipes worth comparing with the in-game tooltip.
